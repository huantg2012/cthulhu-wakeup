Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a restrained reusable card substrate for a dark cosmic-horror card game; a flat 2D raster UI material, NOT a complete illustrated card, no text.
Primary request: one blank vertically oriented card sheet, aspect ratio 2:3, viewed exactly face-on, genuinely transparent outside its subtly torn silhouette. This is the quiet physical carrier beneath diverse existing procedural illustrations: oil-like black-background paintings, white-background watercolor and saturated magazine halftone images. The carrier must NOT impose a competing illustration style.
Material: light neutral unbleached bone paper, luminous enough to separate clearly from an almost-black game environment. Warm-cool balanced pale mineral ivory, not white, not ochre, not gray-green. Rich but exceedingly low-contrast, large-scale matte paper variation; subtle visible long fibres and soft rubbed printing remnants. No photographic lighting or 3D bevel. Flat printed game art.
Composition: one continuous sheet with no header/footer subdivisions or window. The upper 73% should be especially quiet and almost uniform because original artwork will be placed over it. In the bottom 27%, keep the centre-left calm for 3 lines of readable dark text. A very faint, broad ghost of rubbed charcoal at the extreme lower right and bottom edge may suggest old contact with something beyond the picture, but no recognizable picture or conspicuous symbol. Less than 5% contrast in this faint print residue. Edge wear is small, irregular dry-print bites and paper fibres, not a geometrically regular commercial trading-card rectangle. No excessive damage, giant tears, ink splatters or holes. Keep at least 95% of the card area solid and usable.
Constraints: transparent background; single isolated blank sheet occupying most of the portrait canvas; no shadow cast onto an opaque background; no lettering, no symbols, no eyes, no ornaments, no corner decorations, no border strokes, no picture frame, no mat, no circles, no vortex, no rays, no waves, no illustration, no panels. Absolutely no gold foil, glowing edges, fantasy metalwork, embossed stone, shiny plastic or neat luxury business-card finish. Restrained and cohesive enough to disappear behind very different painting styles.
